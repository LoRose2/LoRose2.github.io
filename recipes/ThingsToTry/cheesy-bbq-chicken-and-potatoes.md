# Ingredients (Makes 8 Servings) [^1]

## Chicken Marinade
- 1500g Raw Chicken Breast, cut into small cubes
- 3 Tsp Salt
- 4 Tsp Oregano
- 2.5 Tsp Chilli Flakes
- 5 Tsp Paprika
- 5 Tsp Garlic Powder
- 2 Tbsp Fresh Parsley, chopped
- 6 Tsp Olive Oil

## Roasted Potatoes
- 1500g Raw White/Yellow Potatoes, cubed
- 250g Red Onion, chopped
- 90g Fresh Garlic, chopped
- 4 Tsp Spicy Italian Herb Seasoning (Brand: Schwartz)
- 4 Tsp Paprika
- 4 Tsp Onion Powder
- Avocado Cooking Spray, heavy amount

## BBQ Filling + Garnish
- 250g Reduced Sugar BBQ Sauce
- 120g Low Fat Cheddar
- 120g Shredded Mozzarella
- Grated Parmesan, Parsley for garnish

# Steps

1. Slice chicken breast into small cubes. Add all the seasonings with chopped parsley and olive oil. Mix well until the colour changes, set aside
2. Line a large sheet pan with parchment paper, add cubed potatoes, chopped red onion, chopped garlic cloves, extra seasoning and go heavy with cooking spray. Mix until well coated!
3. Oven roast for 20 mins at 200C/400F until slightly crispy golden. Now spread marinated chicken evenly over the potatoes. Add cooking spray, then back in oven for 25 mins until golden and juicy.
4. Once cooked, while the pan is still hot, add BBQ sauce, cheddar and mozzarella then mix and fold gently until melted and cheesy.
5. Garnish then divide into 8 equal portions and ENJOY!
