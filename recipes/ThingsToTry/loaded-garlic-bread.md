# Ingredients[^1]

## Garlic Bread

- 1/2 French loaf
- 1 lb shaved roast beef
- 3 onions, thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 8 slices provolone

## Garlic Butter

- 3/4 cup butter
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tbsp parsley
- 1/2 tsp salt

## Au Jus

- 3 cups beef broth
- 1 1/2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 1/2 tsp soy sauce
- 3/4 tsp onion powder
- 3/4 tsp black pepper

# Steps

1. Melt 1 tbsp butter in a pan over medium-low heat. Add the thinly sliced onions with the brown sugar and a pinch of salt, stirring occasionally, until deeply golden and caramelised (45–60+ minutes).
2. Mix the garlic butter ingredients.
3. Simmer all the au jus ingredients for 15–20 minutes.
4. Warm the shaved roast beef in the hot au jus for 30–60 seconds.
5. Slice the loaf in half lengthwise, spread the garlic butter over one half, and top with the warmed beef, caramelised onions and cheese.
6. Bake at 400°F for 12 minutes, then broil for 2 minutes until the cheese is golden.
7. Slice into portions and serve with the warm au jus for dipping.
