# Ingredients[^1]

sausage pizza bubble bake
1 package The Sausage Project Italian Herb Chicken Sausage, sliced
1 can large refrigerated biscuits, cut into 6 pieces each
1 jar pizza sauce, 12.3 oz
2 cups shredded mozzarella
1/4–1/2 cup grated parmesan

# Steps
Mix the biscuit pieces with the pizza sauce, about half the sausage, 1 cup mozzarella + parmesan in a greased 9x13. top with the remaining mozzarella + sausage.

Bake at 375°F for 30–35 minutes, or until the biscuits are fully cooked through and the cheese is bubbly. Every oven is a little different, so check the center before taking it out.
