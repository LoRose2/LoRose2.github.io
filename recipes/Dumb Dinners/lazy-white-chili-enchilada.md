30 minutes | 12 servings

# Ingredients [^1]
1 rotisserie chicken, pulled into chunks (about 3 cups)
1 can white beans, drained and rinsed
1 cup frozen fire-roasted corn
1 small can diced green chiles
1 jar salsa verde
½ can green enchilada sauce
½ tub whipped cream cheese
½ packet white chicken chili seasoning
10 corn tortillas
1 cup shredded Monterey Jack
1 cup shredded pepper jack
1 lime, plus crema and cotija to finish (optional) 

# Steps
1. Heat the oven to 375°F and spray a 9x13 dish
1. Pour in the salsa verde and a couple spoonfuls of the enchilada sauce
1. Add the chicken, beans, corn, and green chiles
1. Drop in the whipped cream cheese and sprinkle on the seasoning
1. Tear 8 tortillas into rough quarters right over the pan
1. Stir until the cream cheese disappears and everything’s coated
1. Tear the last 2 tortillas over the top and spoon the rest of the enchilada sauce over them
1. Cover tightly with foil and bake 20 min
1. Uncover, add both cheeses, bake 15 to 20 min until browned at the edges
1. Let it sit 10 min, then squeeze the lime over everything and finish with crema and cotija
