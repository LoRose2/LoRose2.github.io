# Ingredients [^1]

20 oz refrigerated cheese tortellini
12 oz fully cooked chicken sausage, sliced
1 jar Alfredo sauce (15 oz)
½ cup pumpkin purée
½ cup chicken broth
1 tsp garlic powder
1 tsp onion powder
½ tsp Italian seasoning
¼ tsp black pepper
¼ tsp red pepper flakes
A pinch of nutmeg
1 cup shredded mozzarella
¼ cup grated Parmesan

# Steps
Heat the oven to 400°F and spray a 9x13 dish
Add the Alfredo, pumpkin, broth, and all the seasonings to the empty dish and stir until it’s one color
Add the tortellini and sausage, stir to coat
Cover tightly with foil, bake 20 min
Pull the foil and stir, then top with the mozzarella and Parmesan
Bake uncovered 8 to 10 min until bubbly with golden spots
Let it sit 5 min before serving so the sauce thickens
